My research combines analytical methods and numerical exploration to understand stability, transitions, and complex behaviour in nonlinear systems.

Bifurcation & chaos

How does stable behaviour become oscillatory or chaotic? I investigate local bifurcations and the organisation of dynamics in discrete and continuous models.

Delay & memory

Past states can shape present dynamics. I explore how delayed responses and filtered feedback affect stability and generate new dynamical behaviour.
